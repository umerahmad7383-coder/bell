## Description (itch.io, CrazyGames, Newgrounds)

You and up to seven friends are locked inside an abandoned school at night, and the Headmaster is still doing his rounds.

Find three fuses and bring the power back. Break into the Principal's Office for the gate key. Disarm the gate from the Security Office and run. When the gate opens, the final bell rings: every light turns red and you have two minutes to get everyone out before it slams shut for good.

He hears you run. He checks lockers. If he catches one of you, he goes for the friend who stops to help.

- Online co-op for 2–8 players. Make a room and send your friends the code.
- Hunt Mode: one of you plays the Headmaster.
- Proximity voice chat. He can hear you too.
- Four survivor classes: Athlete, Medic, Mechanic and Sneak.
- Four difficulties, plus optional rules like One Strike and Lights Out.
- Hide in lockers and hold your breath, throw bottles, flash him with a camera, return a lost teddy bear to the boy who owned it.
- Works with keyboard and mouse, or an Xbox or PlayStation controller.
- Free. Runs in the browser on a computer (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). No download.

Best with friends, headphones and the lights off.

**Tags:** horror, multiplayer, co-op, first-person, survival horror, atmospheric, 3D, voice chat, browser, school

## Short videos (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels)

Record with OBS Studio (free) and edit with CapCut (free). Play with friends on voice chat; the reactions are the content.

- The jumpscare: someone gets caught and screams. Show the moment, cut fast.
- "He went for the one who stopped to help": a revive gone wrong.
- The final bell: the lights turn red and everyone sprints for the gate.
- Hiding in a locker, holding your breath, while he checks the locker next to yours.
- Hunt Mode: film from the Headmaster's side as your friend walks right past you.

Keep each one 15–30 seconds with the scare or the joke in the first two seconds. Put "free, play in your browser, link in bio" on screen.
